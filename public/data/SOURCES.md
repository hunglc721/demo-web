`vietnam-provinces.json` and `vietnam-wards.json` contain Vietnam's current 34 province/city and 3,321 commune/ward divisions.

Source: [open-admin-data/vietnam-administrative-divisions](https://github.com/open-admin-data/vietnam-administrative-divisions), data snapshot published 2026-09-08.
License: CC BY 4.0. The dataset attributes its administrative data to public General Statistics Office sources; it is an independent project, not a government publication.

Vietnam's current checkout address hierarchy has two levels (province/city → commune/ward); district-level units were abolished in the 2025 reform. This checkout uses the current hierarchy rather than the former 63-province/district model.
